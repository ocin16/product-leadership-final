# Product Strategy & OKRs: Meridian Foundations

> Module 1 · Write a Strategy That Can Survive a Test

## 1. Playing to Win cascade

| Question | Your choice |
|---|---|
| **Winning aspiration** | Enterprise customers run complex projects with less overhead, because field crews record the job site in Meridian, which is quicker than texting. |
| **Where to play** | Field crews at top-100 general contractors in the construction businesses, the goal is to chase new logos. We don’t compete for projects under $10M for now. |
| **How to win** | We know which use cases matter to large customers, like multi-site management. Being the system of record for 38 of the top 100 gives us lock-in, data to harvest and a big customer network to leverage. |
| **Capabilities required** | Find the use cases that matter most in the data we have and fix the data journey from field to office, so missing data doesn’t choke the value the software delivers. We must be obsessed with our target customers, understand their work environment and solve the problems that matter most to them. Test, learn and deliver quickly. |
| **Management systems** | Split customers into tiers (eg. strategic, enterprise, breadth) with sales KPIs for each;  Track how many field users are active and review it every quarter. |

## 2. Your one hard no

> **Hard no:** Field features that take longer than a text; More analytics, dashboards and ERP integrations for now. Say no to features built for small companies.

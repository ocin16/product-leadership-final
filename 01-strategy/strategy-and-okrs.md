# Product Strategy & OKRs: Meridian Foundations

> Module 1 · Write a Strategy That Can Survive a Test

## 1. Playing to Win cascade

| Question | Your choice |
|---|---|
| **Winning aspiration** | Enterprise customers run complex projects with less overhead, because field crews record the job site in Meridian, which is quicker than texting. |
| **Where to play** | Field crews at top-100 general contractors in the construction businesses, the goal is to chase new logos. We don’t compete for projects under $10M for now. |
| **How to win** | We know which use cases matter to large customers, like multi-site management. Being the system of record for 38 of the top 100 gives us a lot of data to harvest and a deep integration into the systems of the customers. |
| **Capabilities required** | Find the use cases that matter most in the data we have and fix the data journey from field to office, so missing data doesn’t choke the value the software delivers. We must be obsessed with our target customers, understand their work environment and solve the problems that matter most to them. Test, learn and deliver quickly. Ensure all relevant stakeholders have easy access to Meridian. |
| **Management systems** | Split customers into tiers (eg. strategic, mid-market, small companies) with sales KPIs for each;  The share of projects with daily field logs in Meridian. |

## 2. Your one hard no

> **Hard no:** Say no to features built for small companies but take them as a "easy win" if they come for free; More analytics, dashboards and ERP integrations for now.

## 3. OKR cascade

> **Objective:** Make Meridian the place field crews record the job site, not the group chat.
>
> - **KR1:** The share of active customer projects with daily field logs in Meridian goes from about 10% to 40% this quarter.
> - **KR2:** Per project, the share of active field users  in Meridian goes from about 5% to 40% this quarter.
> - **KR3:** The share of field logs captured on site on mobile, rather than typed up later in the office, goes from about 10% to 50% this quarter.
